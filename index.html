ABOUT US

Built on the fire of God, carried by His glory.

House of God Fire and Glory Ministry began as a small prayer gathering with one conviction: that God's presence changes everything. That conviction still shapes everything we do today.

Word-centered

Every gathering is built on Scripture taught plainly, so that truth can be lived out, not just heard.

Spirit-led worship

We make room for the presence of God to move in our services — not performance, but encounter.

Family in faith

Small groups, prayer teams, and mentorship mean you're never walking your journey alone.

Reaching out

From local outreach to global missions, we carry what we receive here out into the world.

LEADERSHIP

Called to shepherd this house.

Apostle Abraham Wealth Abokondu

Lead Pastor

Apostle Abraham Wealth Abokondu has led House of God Fire and Glory Ministry since its founding, carrying a deep conviction for prayer, the Word, and the manifest presence of God. His teaching is direct, Scripture-rooted, and aimed at helping believers live out their faith with everyday courage.

Beyond the pulpit, he gives himself to mentoring emerging leaders and shepherding the congregation through seasons both joyful and difficult.

Pastor Serah Abokondu

First Lady & Co-Pastor

Pastor Serah Abokondu serves alongside her husband with a heart for discipleship, women's ministry, and pastoral care. Her warmth and wisdom have shaped countless lives in this congregation.

She leads our women's fellowship and counseling ministry, and is often found praying with members of the congregation before and after every service.
